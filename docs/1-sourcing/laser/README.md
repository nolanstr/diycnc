# Laser-cut steel

The steel skeletons: plates that slot together. Send these to a laser-cutting service, Metal Supermarkets, or anyone with a CNC plasma or router table. Mild steel (A36 or 1018), units mm. Holes to be tapped are drawn at the tap drill size; tap them before assembly. Keep slots and tabs clean of dross: they should slide together by hand.
